Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective September 23, 2026

Slangback is designed with privacy in mind. The Slangback iOS app does not collect personal data from its users.

Data collection

Slangback does not require an account and does not collect or store personal information, search activity, usage analytics, advertising identifiers, or location data.

Tracking and advertising

Slangback does not track users across apps or websites and does not include advertising or advertising trackers.

Third-party analytics

Slangback does not use third-party analytics services that collect data from the app.

Children's privacy

Because Slangback does not collect personal data, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If Slangback's data practices change, this policy and the app's App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before those changes are released.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy can be directed to the Slangback developer through the support contact associated with the app's App Store listing.
