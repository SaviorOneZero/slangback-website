Need a hand?

Slangback is a lightweight slang discovery and reference app for iPhone.

Quick help

If something doesn't look right, first make sure you're running the latest version of Slangback and iOS, then close and reopen the app.

Questions or problems

For support, bug reports, corrections, or other questions, contact the Slangback developer through the support contact associated with the app's App Store listing.

Privacy

Slangback is designed to work without accounts, advertising, or tracking. Read the privacy policy for details.
