Slangback Support

Slangback is a slang reference app for iPhone.

If the app is acting up

Update Slangback and iOS, then quit and reopen the app. If the problem sticks around, send us an email with your iPhone model, iOS version, and what happened.

Found something wrong?

Open the entry and tap Suggest a correction. Slangback will draft an email with the entry and version details filled in. Nothing is sent until you choose Send.

You can also email a correction directly.

Privacy

Slangback works without an account, advertising, or tracking. The privacy policy explains what happens when you open a source, share a card, or send a correction.
